## Most Features Are Completely Free

DeepShare is an open-source browser extension. The following features are **permanently free**:

- ✓ Share DeepSeek conversations as images
- ✓ Customize watermarks on shared images
- ✓ One-click copy formulas from AI conversations
- ✓ Export Gemini and ChatGPT to Markdown
- ✓ Export Gemini Deep Research to Markdown
- ✓ Clean, ad-free interface

## Word Document Conversion Pricing

**Only the Word document conversion feature requires payment**, as it uses server resources for high-quality typesetting and format conversion.

### Notes

- **Auto-renewal**: All plans auto-renew. You can cancel anytime before the next billing date.
- **API Key**: After purchase, your API key is displayed immediately and sent to your email.

### Upgrades

To upgrade, simply subscribe to a higher-tier plan — Creem automatically charges only the prorated difference based on remaining days:

> **Difference = (New plan price − Old plan price) × (Days remaining / Billing cycle days)**
>
> *Example: You're on Standard Monthly ($4.99/mo) with 15 days left in the 30-day cycle, and upgrade to Pro Monthly ($9.99/mo). Creem charges: ($9.99 − $4.99) × (15 / 30) = **$2.50** instead of the full $9.99.*

## How to Purchase and Use

### Purchase Steps

1. Visit the [Subscription Page](https://ds.rick216.cn/en/price.html)
2. Enter your email address (used to bind your account and receive your API Key)
3. After payment, your API Key is displayed immediately and sent to your email

### Configure the Extension

1. Click the DeepShare icon in the browser toolbar
2. Enter the **API Key** you received in the settings
3. You will then see the "Export Word" button on supported AI chat pages
