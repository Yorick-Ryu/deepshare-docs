### Rules & Notes

- **Validity**: Starting from the last purchase/renewal, the API Key's total validity is extended by **1 year**.
- **Quota Priority**: The system first consumes the subscription plan's "daily quota"; after the daily quota is exhausted, it consumes "pay-as-you-go credits".
- **No Auto-renewal**: All plans are purchased manually and will not auto-charge upon expiry.
- **Plan Upgrade**: Plan upgrades are supported; remaining time is **converted at a 2:1 ratio** to the new plan.

## How to Purchase and Use

### Purchase Steps

1. Visit the [Plan Purchase Page](https://ds.rick216.cn/purchase-en.html) or [Credits Purchase Page](https://ds.rick216.cn/purchase-en.html)
2. Enter your email address (for receiving the API Key)
3. After payment, the system will automatically display and send the API Key to your email

### Configure the Extension

1. Click the DeepShare icon in the browser toolbar
2. Enter the **API Key** you received in the settings
3. You will then see the "Export Word" button on supported AI chat pages

[View detailed usage instructions](/en/usage)

## Check Quota & Renew

### Check Quota

You can check your remaining conversions in the following ways:
- **Extension Popup**: Click the browser icon to directly view the current key's remaining conversions and expiry date.
- **Online Query**: Enter your Key in the "Check Remaining Quota" section on the [purchase page](https://ds.rick216.cn/purchase-en.html) to view detailed breakdown.

### Renew & Top Up

When your quota is running low or about to expire:
- Visit the [Renewal/Top-up Page](https://ds.rick216.cn/renew.html)
- Enter your existing API Key to renew; the corresponding quota or time will be added to your account.

**Note**: Renewal extends the validity by 1 additional year from the current expiry date.
