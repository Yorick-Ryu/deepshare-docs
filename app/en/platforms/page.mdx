DeepShare supports multiple major AI platforms:

| AI Platform | Copy Formulas | Export to Word | Screenshot |
|-------------|:-------------:|:--------------:|:----------:|
| DeepSeek | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

*Kimi officially supports formula copying — right-click a formula to copy it.
