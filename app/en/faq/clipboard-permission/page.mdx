# How to Enable Clipboard Permission

<Callout type="info">

Enable clipboard read permission on the DeepSeek chat page, not on this documentation website.

</Callout>

When DeepShare shows "Failed to save, please enable clipboard read permission" while exporting a Word document from DeepSeek, it means the browser has blocked the web page from reading clipboard content. Follow the steps below to enable the permission.

<Steps>

### Open the DeepSeek chat page

Open DeepSeek and enter the conversation you want to export.

### Click the clipboard icon on the right side of the address bar

![Click the clipboard icon on the right side of the address bar](/images/deepseek/clipboard-permission-icon.png)

### Change clipboard permission to allow

Set DeepSeek's clipboard read permission to "Continue allowing this site to see your clipboard", then click "Done".

![Allow DeepSeek to read the clipboard](/images/deepseek/clipboard-permission-allow.png)

</Steps>

After enabling the permission, export again.
