# Copy Formulas from AI Chats

<Callout>Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation).</Callout>

## Copy Formula to Word

Using DeepSeek as an example — just click a formula to copy it, then paste it into Word.

![DeepSeek-Share](/images/deepseek/copy-formula.png)

After pasting, select the formula and choose "Professional" to make the formula format more consistent and attractive!

![formula-format](/images/formula/format.png)

## Copy Formula to WPS

Copying formulas to WPS is slightly more complex, but using keyboard shortcuts makes it easy once you're familiar.

<Steps>

### Change the formula format

WPS requires LaTeX format. Click the DeepShare extension icon in the browser toolbar, click "Formula Copy Settings", and select `LaTeX` as the copy format.

<Callout>If you don't know how to open that interface, [see here](/en/installation#pin-deepshare-to-toolbar).</Callout>

![DeepSeek-Share](/images/formula/select-latex.png)

### Copy the formula

Click the formula to copy it, then paste it into WPS.

![DeepSeek-Share](/images/deepseek/copy-formula.png)

### Insert a formula box

Click the top menu bar "Insert" → "Formula" → "Insert Formula" or "Insert LaTeX"

Windows shortcut: `Alt + =` to insert a formula box;

Mac shortcut: `Command + Shift + F` to insert a formula box.

![insert-formula](/images/wps/insert-formula.png)

### Paste the LaTeX code

After pasting the LaTeX code, press `Enter` at the end of the formula box to automatically convert it to a standard formula.

![insert-formula](/images/wps/paste-formula.gif)

</Steps>

Reference the official WPS tutorial: [WPS now supports LaTeX formula conversion!](https://forum.wps.cn/topic/53595)

## Copy Formula to MathType

Copy formulas to Word or WPS that has the MathType formula editor installed.

<Steps>

### Change the formula format

MathType supports LaTeX format. Click the DeepShare extension icon in the browser toolbar, click "Formula Copy Settings", and select `LaTeX` as the copy format.

<Callout>If you don't know how to open that interface, [see here](/en/installation#pin-deepshare-to-toolbar).</Callout>

![DeepSeek-Share](/images/formula/select-latex.png)

### Copy and paste to MathType

Click the formula to copy it, then open the MathType formula editor in Word or WPS and paste the formula.

</Steps>

## Copy Formula to Feishu, Notion, Obsidian

These note-taking apps support Markdown formula syntax (`$$formula$$`). DeepShare provides dedicated Markdown format support.

<Steps>

### Change the formula format

Click the DeepShare extension icon in the browser toolbar, click "Formula Copy Settings", and select `Markdown` as the copy format.

<Callout>If you don't know how to open that interface, [see here](/en/installation#pin-deepshare-to-toolbar).</Callout>

![select-markdown](/images/formula/select-markdown.png)

### Copy the formula

Click the formula to copy it. The formula will be copied to the clipboard in `$$..$$` format.

![DeepSeek-Share](/images/deepseek/copy-formula.png)

### Paste into the note-taking app

Paste directly in Feishu/Lark, Notion, or Obsidian — the formula will render automatically.

</Steps>

## FAQ

### Is the formula copying feature free?

The formula copying feature is permanently free. Formulas are extracted from the webpage and processed locally — they are not uploaded to any server. The formula copying related code is [open source](https://github.com/Yorick-Ryu/deep-share).

### After clicking a formula, the copied content doesn't match the selected format?

Another browser extension with formula copying functionality (e.g., Gemini Voyager) may be installed and causing a conflict. Try disabling the other extension, then refresh the page and try again.

### There's a space after pasting the formula?

For some formulas, a space may appear after pasting. Select the formula box and press `Space` to resolve this.

![formula-format](/images/formula/space.png)

### The formula format after pasting doesn't match the webpage?

After pasting, select the formula and choose "Professional" to make the formula format more consistent and attractive!

![formula-format](/images/formula/format.png)

### How do I change the formula font?

Reference: [How to change the formula format to Times New Roman font in Word?](https://www.zhihu.com/question/63424892)

### The brace in a piecewise function becomes short after pasting to Word?

This issue usually occurs in Word on Mac. You need to manually replace the formula.

<Steps>

#### Paste and delete

First paste the formula twice, and delete the part after the equals sign in one of the formulas.

![formula-format](/images/formula/piecewise-function-delete.png)

#### Insert a new brace

Place the cursor after the equals sign in the formula, then click Formula → Brackets → Curly Brace in the top toolbar. The correct brace is now inserted.

![formula-format](/images/formula/piecewise-function-insert.png)

#### Move the sub-branch

Copy and paste the original sub-branches of the formula after the new brace.

![formula-format](/images/formula/piecewise-function-move.png)

#### Done

You can delete the previous formula.

![formula-format](/images/formula/piecewise-function-fixed.png)

</Steps>
