# Gemini Canvas Export to Markdown

<Callout>

Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation). Refresh Gemini after installation.

</Callout>

Gemini's **Canvas** is very useful — you can continuously iterate and refine generated content there. You can also export it to Markdown with one click.

The method below also supports exporting Gemini's **Deep Research** to Markdown.

![Click share button](/images/gemini/click-share-btn.png)
