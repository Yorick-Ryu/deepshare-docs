# Gemini Canvas Export to Word

<Callout>

Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation). Refresh Gemini after installation.

This feature requires purchasing an API Key. [View pricing](/en/pricing).

</Callout>

Gemini's **Canvas** is very useful — you can continuously iterate and refine generated content there. You can also export it to Word with one click.

The method below also supports exporting Gemini's **Deep Research** to Word.

<Steps>

### Click the share button

![Click share button](/images/gemini/click-share-btn.png)

### Click "Save as Word"

![Save as word](/images/gemini/save-canvas.png)

### View the converted document

The document will be automatically downloaded. Open it with Word or WPS.

![Word](/images/gemini/word-canvas.png)

</Steps>
