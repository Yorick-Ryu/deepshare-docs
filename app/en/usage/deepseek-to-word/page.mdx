# DeepSeek Export to Word

<Callout>

Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation).

This feature requires purchasing Word document conversion quota. [View pricing](/en/pricing).

</Callout>

There are two ways to export to Word.

## Method 1: Export a single response

Click the "Save as Word" button directly.

![Save as word](/images/deepseek/save-as-word.png)

## Method 2: Export multiple responses

This method allows you to export multiple conversation turns at once.

<Steps>

### Click the share button in the top right of the page

![DeepSeek Share](/images/deepseek/deepseek-share.png)

### Click "Save as Word"

![Save Docx](/images/deepseek/save-docx.png)

### View the exported document

The document will be automatically downloaded. Open it with Word or WPS.

![Word](/images/deepseek/word.png)

</Steps>
