# Gemini Deep Research Export to Word

<Callout>

Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation). Refresh Gemini after installation.

This feature requires purchasing an API Key. [View pricing](/en/pricing).

This feature only supports the official Gemini website. Mirror sites can contact WeChat yorick_cn for customization (Pro users only).

</Callout>

Gemini's **Deep Research** is very useful — you can also export it to Word with one click.

## Preview

The exported Word document is beautifully formatted, optimized for academic papers, and **supports formulas, tables, and side table of contents**.

![Deep Research Word](/images/gemini/deep-research-word.png)

## Steps

<Steps>

### Click the "Share and export" button

![Click share button](/images/gemini/click-share-and-export-btn.png)

### Click "Save as Word"

![Save as word](/images/gemini/save-deep-research.png)

</Steps>

## Export Sources

DeepShare supports exporting Gemini Deep Research to Word including citation sources.

You can choose whether to include sources in the settings:

![ExportGeminiSources](/images/gemini/export-gemini-sources-option.png)

The extension also supports Gemini [**Canvas** Export to Word](/en/usage/gemini-canvas-to-word).
