# Gemini Deep Research Export to Markdown

<Callout>

Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation). Refresh Gemini after installation.

</Callout>

Gemini's **Deep Research** is very useful — you can also export it to Markdown with one click.

The method below also supports exporting Gemini's **Canvas** to Markdown.

<Steps>

### Click the "Share and export" button

![Click share button](/images/gemini/click-share-and-export-btn.png)

### Click "Save as Markdown"

![Save as Markdown](/images/gemini/save-deep-research.png)

</Steps>

## Export Sources

DeepShare supports exporting Gemini Deep Research to Markdown including citation sources.

You can choose whether to include sources in the settings:

![ExportGeminiSources](/images/gemini/export-gemini-sources-option.png)

DeepShare also supports [Gemini Deep Research Export to Word](gemini-deep-research-to-word) and [Canvas Export to Word](/en/usage/gemini-canvas-to-word).
