# Gemini Export to Word

<Callout>

Please ensure the DeepShare extension is installed in your browser. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation). Refresh Gemini after installation.

This feature requires purchasing an API Key. [View pricing](/en/pricing).

This feature only supports the official Gemini website. Mirror sites can contact WeChat yorick_cn for customization (Pro users only).

</Callout>

## Export a single AI response

Click the **Save as Word** button below the AI response.

![Save as word](/images/gemini/save-as-word.png)

## Export the full conversation

Click "More" in the top right of the conversation, then select **Save as Word** from the dropdown.

![Save as word](/images/gemini/save.png)

## View the exported document

The document will be automatically downloaded. Open it with Word or WPS.

![Word](/images/gemini/word.png)

DeepShare also supports:

- [Gemini Deep Research Export to Markdown](gemini-deep-research-to-md)
- [Gemini Deep Research Export to Word](gemini-deep-research-to-word)

- [Google AI Studio Export to Word](/en/usage/aistudio-to-word)
