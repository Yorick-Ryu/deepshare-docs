# DeepShare - Export AI Chats to Word Extension

DeepShare is a powerful browser extension that helps you manage and share AI conversation content more effectively.

### Core Features

- **Export to Word** - Convert AI responses to beautifully formatted Word documents with one click, preserving formulas and tables perfectly
- **Copy Formulas** - Click any math formula in AI conversations to copy its LaTeX or MathML code, ready to paste into Word or WPS
- **AI Chat Screenshots** - Easily capture full conversation content (DeepSeek exclusive feature)

### Install the Extension

Please choose the corresponding app store **based on your browser** to install DeepShare

- [Edge Add-ons Store](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/deepshare/pdccjnppfegekpnhfljbngammgfbcofm)
- [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/omnaecaamcabmnbjnpjpecoaalfgidop)
- [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/deepshare/)

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> After installation, please refresh any open AI chat pages for the extension to take effect. For more information, see the [Installation Guide](/en/installation)

### Supported Platforms

DeepShare supports DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Gemini, and many other major AI platforms. See the [supported platforms list](/en/platforms) for details.

### Design Philosophy

DeepShare is designed to reuse the original webpage's styles as much as possible, minimizing intrusiveness. Compared to large language models, the extension does a very small amount of work — we don't want to steal the show.
