# Gemini导出Word

<Callout>

请确保浏览器已安装 DeepShare 插件，未安装请查看[安装指南](/installation)，安装后请刷新Gemini

本功能需要购买API-KEY，[点此查看定价](/pricing)

</Callout>


<Steps>

### 点击「保存为Word」按钮

![Save as word](/images/gemini/save-as-word.png)

### 查看导出的文档

文档会自动下载，使用Word打开查看文档

![Word](/images/gemini/word.png)
</Steps>

DeepShare还支持[Gemini Deep Reaserch导出Word](gemini-deep-research-to-word)和[Canvas模式导出Word](/usage/gemini-canvas-to-word)
